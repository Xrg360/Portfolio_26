## Outline

- The problem: many platforms, near-identical wording; similarity search mixes them up.
- The approach: let the model navigate (search, table of contents, read a section, delegate extraction).
- The rule: answers may only cite documents the model actually read.
- Trade-offs: more model calls and latency, in exchange for platform-specific answers.
